# Customer Refund Policy (Version 1.0 - Archived 2024)

## 1. Eligibility Window
Customers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of original purchase date.
Requests initiated after day 14 are strictly ineligible for refunds and may only receive store credit.

## 2. Restocking & Processing Fees
All hardware and physical product returns incur a mandatory $15.00 restocking fee deducted from the total refund amount.
Digital software subscriptions are non-refundable once the license key has been activated.

## 3. Processing Timeline
Approved refunds are processed via the original payment method within 10 to 14 business days.
